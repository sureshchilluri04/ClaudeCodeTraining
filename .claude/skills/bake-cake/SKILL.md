# Bake a Cake

Step-by-step guidance for preparing a basic cake from scratch, plus common variations and troubleshooting.

## When to Apply

- User asks "how do I bake a cake" or requests a cake recipe
- User wants to plan ingredients/quantities for a cake (e.g., scaling for a party)
- User asks about frosting, layering, or troubleshooting a cake (sunken middle, dry texture, etc.)

## Basic Vanilla Cake (serves ~8-10, two 9-inch rounds)

### Ingredients
- 2 1/4 cups (280g) all-purpose flour
- 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup (225g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 3/4 cups (350g) granulated sugar
- 4 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup (240ml) whole milk, room temperature

### Steps
1. **Preheat & prep**: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round cake pans (or line with parchment).
2. **Dry mix**: Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt together in a bowl. Set aside.
3. **Cream butter & sugar**: Beat softened butter and sugar together until light and fluffy (3-5 minutes).
4. **Add eggs**: Beat in eggs one at a time, then mix in vanilla extract.
5. **Combine**: Alternate adding the dry mix and milk to the butter mixture in 3 additions (dry, milk, dry, milk, dry), mixing just until combined — do not overmix.
6. **Bake**: Divide batter evenly between pans. Bake 25-30 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
7. **Cool**: Cool in pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely before frosting.
8. **Frost & assemble**: Once fully cool, level tops if needed, stack with frosting between layers, then frost the outside.

## Simple Buttercream Frosting
- 1 cup (225g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3-4 cups (360-480g) powdered sugar
- 2-3 tbsp milk or heavy cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

Beat butter until creamy, gradually add powdered sugar, then vanilla, salt, and milk/cream a splash at a time until smooth and spreadable.

## Common Variations
- **Chocolate cake**: Replace 1/2 cup flour with 1/2 cup cocoa powder, add 1/2 cup hot water/coffee for moisture.
- **Gluten-free**: Substitute a 1:1 gluten-free flour blend.
- **Egg-free**: Replace each egg with 1/4 cup unsweetened applesauce or a flax egg.

## Troubleshooting
- **Cake sinks in the middle**: Oven too cool, underbaked, or too much leavening — check oven temp with a thermometer.
- **Dry/dense texture**: Overmixed batter or overbaked — mix just until combined, check doneness a few minutes early.
- **Cake sticks to pan**: Grease and flour pans thoroughly, or use parchment rounds.
- **Uneven layers**: Use a kitchen scale to divide batter evenly between pans.

## Scaling
To scale for more servings, multiply all ingredient quantities proportionally and adjust pan size/count and bake time accordingly (larger pans need longer, lower-temp bakes to avoid burning the edges before the center sets).
